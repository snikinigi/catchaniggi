Export voyages or port calls from your AIS provider (MarineTraffic, VesselFinder, Datalastic and similar) and load them here. Include a header row. Columns, in any order: name, type, flag, imo, from, to, departed, arrived (or eta), status. Port names or UN/LOCODEs such as OMSOH and AEJEA both work. CSV, tab-separated or a JSON array.

Fishing, yacht and naval vessels are dropped on import. AIS destinations are typed by crews, so cross-check against port-call records.
